## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Blinkit's business performance using Power BI. It explores key business areas including sales, customer behaviour, financial performance, delivery operations, and strategic performance indicators.

The objective is to transform raw business data into meaningful insights through interactive dashboards, helping stakeholders understand performance, identify trends, and make data-driven business decisions.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze overall business and sales performance.
- Understand customer behaviour and purchasing patterns.
- Evaluate product and category performance.
- Examine financial performance and key metrics.
- Assess delivery efficiency and operational performance.
- Identify business trends and opportunities for improvement.

## Dashboard Modules

The Power BI dashboard is organized into six analytical sections:

| Dashboard | Key Focus |
|---|---|
| Executive Overview | Overall business performance and KPIs |
| Customer Dashboard | Customer behaviour and segmentation |
| Sales Performance | Sales trends and product performance |
| Financial Performance | Revenue and financial metrics |
| Delivery & Operations | Delivery efficiency and operational analysis |
| Strategic Insights | Business insights and performance opportunities |

## Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards and data visualization.
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX:** Calculated measures and analytical metrics.
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV:** Data handling and preparation.
