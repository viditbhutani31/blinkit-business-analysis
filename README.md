An end-to-end Business Intelligence portfolio project analyzing Blinkit's business performance across sales, customers, products, financial performance, delivery operations, and marketing efficiency using Power BI.

> **Note:** This is an independent portfolio analysis based on supplied project datasets and is not official Blinkit company reporting.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to transform multiple interconnected datasets into an interactive business intelligence solution that supports data-driven decision-making.

The dashboard explores key business areas while maintaining consistent KPI definitions across different data grains.

### Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
- Understand customer behaviour and segmentation.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Evaluate gross profit and profitability margins.
- Monitor delivery efficiency and operational performance.
- Analyze marketing expenditure and return on advertising spend.
- Translate business data into actionable insights.

## 📁 Dataset Description

The project uses multiple datasets representing different business functions:

| Dataset | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Orders | Transaction-level order information |
| Order Items | Product quantities and unit prices |
| Products | Product, category, brand and margin information |
| Customers | Customer master and segment information |
| Customer Feedback | Ratings, sentiment and feedback |
| Delivery Performance | Delivery status, time and location |
| Inventory | Inventory and stock information |
| Marketing Performance | Marketing spend and channel performance |

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards and data visualization.
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning, transformation and preparation.
- **DAX:** Calculated measures and business KPIs.
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data handling and preparation.
- **Data Modelling:** Relationships, cardinality and filter context.

**Important metric clarification:** Order-level revenue (11.01M) and product revenue (4.97M) are calculated at different data grains. They represent different business measures and should not be treated as interchangeable.

The portfolio's initial summary page contains some conflicting financial figures compared with its detailed financial page. The detailed-page values are presented above and should be reconciled against the underlying Power BI measures before external reporting.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

**Customer Insights**
- 2,172 distinct customers appear in the order data.
- Average customer rating is 3.34/5.
- Customer feedback indicates opportunities to improve customer experience.

**Sales Insights**
- Product revenue stands at 4.97M.
- Pet Treats is identified as the top-selling product.
- The top 10 products contribute approximately 38% of product revenue.

**Operational Insights**
- 69.4% of deliveries are completed on time.
- 30.6% are classified as delayed.
- City-level analysis highlights geographical variations in delivery performance.

**Financial Insights**
- Gross margin is reported at 27.4%.
- Gross profit is reported at 1.36M.
- Marketing ROAS is reported at 1.97x.

## 🚀 Analytical Workflow

1. **Data Preparation:** Cleaning, transforming and standardizing source datasets.
2. **Data Modelling:** Establishing relationships between business tables.
3. **DAX Development:** Creating reusable measures for revenue, profit, margins and ROAS.
4. **Dashboard Development:** Building interactive visuals, KPI cards and analytical pages.
5. **Business Analysis:** Interpreting results and identifying opportunities for improvement.

## 📌 Project Takeaway

This project demonstrates how Power BI can be used to connect operational and financial data, monitor business performance, and convert raw datasets into meaningful business insights.

It combines business analytics, financial analysis and data visualization to support better decision-making.
